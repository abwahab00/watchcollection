HOW THEY WORK

Watch Technology

Quartz

A battery sends current through a quartz crystal that vibrates at a steady rate, giving accurate timekeeping at a low cost.

Mechanical

A wound spring drives a set of gears. Each watch is assembled and adjusted by hand.

Automatic

A mechanical watch that winds itself from the natural motion of your wrist.

Smart

A touchscreen wearable that connects to your phone for notifications, fitness tracking and apps.
